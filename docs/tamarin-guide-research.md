# Tamarin guide research

Checked 8 September 2026. Sources are also linked beside each public entry. Descriptions are paraphrases; operator marketing claims are not independent endorsements.

Durations are editorial door-to-door planning estimates from Tamarin. The on-site durations attributed to operators are identified in the notes. Exact road times, admission prices and transfer inclusions have not been verified. The $25 car-hire figure is the author's rough budget, not a current quote.

The planner uses the same descriptions and excludes unresolved entries. It must not invent prices, transfers, opening hours or guaranteed wildlife sightings.

## Casela Nature Parks

Safari & wildlife park. The safari bus takes you among giraffes, rhinos, antelopes and ostriches. The rest of the park has big cats, giant tortoises and aviaries. Allow time for the safari departure; check which animal encounters and rides your ticket includes.

- [Casela](https://caselaparks.com/)
- [Safari bus](https://caselaparks.com/activity/safari-bus/)

## Tamarind Falls (7 Cascades)

Guided waterfall hike. Also called Tamarind Falls or 7 Cascades. A half-day guided route takes 3–4 hours on foot and visits part of the falls; the full route takes 6–7 hours of walking. Choose the route with your guide, rather than assuming all seven fit into a morning.

- [Jay 7 Cascades](https://jay7cascades.com/services/)

## Ebony Forest, Chamarel

Native forest & conservation. A forest restoration reserve with endemic birds, a raised walkway and views from Sublime Point. Walking routes take 1–4 hours on site; Piton Canot is the longer extension. There is also a two-hour guided jeep visit that needs booking.

- [Ebony Forest walks](https://www.ebonyforest.com/visit-ebony/take-a-hike/)
- [Jeep visit](https://www.ebonyforest.com/visit-ebony/take-a-drive/)

## Chamarel: Seven Coloured Earth & waterfall

Coloured earth & waterfall. The Seven Coloured Earth geopark includes the dunes, waterfall viewpoints and giant tortoises. The rum distillery is a separate nearby visit. Choose your stops before setting off; adding the distillery and a long Ebony Forest walk makes this a full day.

- [Chamarel geopark](https://chamarel7colouredearth.com/)
- [Rhumerie de Chamarel](https://www.rhumeriedechamarel.com/en/)

## Le Morne Brabant hike

Mountain hike. A mountain walk with lagoon views. The upper route is steep and exposed and ends at the cross below the summit. Check current trail access with the heritage authority before arranging a guide; the upper section is unsuitable for young children or anyone uncomfortable with heights.

- [Le Morne Heritage Trust](https://lemorneheritage.govmu.org/lmh/)
- [Trail information](https://lemorneheritage.govmu.org/lmh/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/General-practical-information.pdf)

## Le Morne beach and lagoon

Beach & lagoon. Public beach below Le Morne Brabant, with the lagoon on one side and the mountain behind you. This entry is for a few hours at the beach. Emba Filao is on the beachfront, so lunch there fits into the same visit.

- [Beach Authority](https://www.beachauthority.mu/pdf/beaches/List2025.pdf)
- [Emba Filao](https://www.luxislandresorts.com/media/7145/integrated-annual-report-lir.pdf)

## Black River Gorges

National park & hiking. Native forest, endemic birds and routes of very different difficulty. A viewpoint visit is a shorter outing; the official Machabée trail from Pétrin to the Lower Gorges is a strenuous 10 km route. Choose the trail and entrance first, then arrange transport for its endpoint.

- [National Parks Service](https://npcs.govmu.org/Pages/National%20Parks/Black-River-Gorges-National-Parks.aspx)

## Vallée des Couleurs (Vallé)

Zipline & adventure park. Now called Vallé Advenature Park. Come for ziplines, quad or buggy rides and the Nepalese suspension bridge, with waterfalls and coloured earth around the park. Choose a ride package before visiting; age, height and weight limits differ by activity.

- [Vallé](https://vallepark.com/)
- [Ride requirements](https://vallepark.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/AGEWEIGHTHEIGHT-REQUIREMENTS.pdf)

## South coast drive

Coastal drive & stops. Head towards Souillac for the cliffs at Gris Gris and La Roche qui Pleure, with Rochester Falls as a separate stop. Leave time to get out of the car, walk and have lunch. Pick a few stops along this coast rather than trying to cross the whole south in one day.

- [Mauritius Tourism](https://mauritiusnow.com/mauritius-map/south-mauritius/)
- [Gris Gris & La Roche qui Pleure](https://mauritiusnow.com/blog/things-to-do/gris-gris-beach/)

## Ile aux Cerfs

Island beach day. An east-coast leisure island with beaches, watersports, restaurants and a golf course. You can take the shuttle from Pointe Maurice or book a longer boat excursion. From Tamarin, allow a full day for the cross-island journey, boat transfers and time on the beach.

- [Île aux Cerfs](https://www.ileauxcerfsleisureisland.com/)

## Port Louis

Markets & city walk. Start at the Central Market for fruit, spices and food stalls, then walk to the Caudan Waterfront for the harbour, craft market and lunch. This is a city visit on foot once you arrive. Leave extra time for traffic on the way in and out.

- [Mauritius Tourism](https://tourism.govmu.org/Pages/Services/Tourist-Sites.aspx)
- [Caudan craft market](https://caudan.com/craft-markets/)

## Turtle snorkelling, Le Morne

Turtle snorkelling. Some west-coast lagoon trips include snorkelling where sea turtles may be seen around Le Morne. Sightings depend on the animals and conditions. Ask which route you are booking: a short snorkelling outing and a lagoon cruise with lunch take different amounts of time.

- [Pelagic lagoon trip](https://www.pelagic-mauritius.com/boat-lagoon-trip)

## Snorkelling

Guided reef snorkelling. A boat outing to west-coast reef sites, with the crew choosing stops for the conditions. Book a dedicated snorkelling trip if that is your priority; a dolphin or lunch cruise may only include a short stop. Confirm equipment, meeting point and return time.

- [ECOCEAN snorkelling](https://www.boatexcursionsmauritius.com/snorkeling.php)

## Diving

Scuba diving. Book through a dive centre and state your experience when enquiring. An introductory session and a trip for certified divers have different requirements and schedules. West-coast centres include Blue Lungs at Le Morne; confirm the centre, dive site and equipment before planning transport.

- [Blue Lungs centre](https://bluelungsdiving.com/en/blue-lungs-diving-centre/)
- [Mauritius Scuba Diving Association](https://msda.mu/en/)

## Dolphin watching

Dolphin watching by boat. An early boat trip off Tamarin and Black River to look for wild dolphins. Book an observation trip if you want to stay on the boat, since many packages also advertise swimming. Sightings are not guaranteed; leave the afternoon free after the early start.

- [Whale Dream](https://www.whalewatchingmauritius.com/)

## Whale watching

Offshore whale watching. Sperm whales live off the west coast year-round; humpbacks visit during the austral winter migration. These trips head farther offshore than lagoon outings, so sea conditions matter. Confirm departure and trip length with the operator, and keep the rest of the day quiet.

- [Whale Dream](https://www.whalewatchingmauritius.com/)
- [Dolswim whale watching](https://www.mauritiusdolphinswim.com/whale-watching)

## Tandem paragliding

Tandem flight. A flight with a pilot, with the launch site chosen for the weather. Mauritius Tandem advertises about 30–35 minutes for its classic flight and uses sites including Le Morne and Chamarel. Confirm the site before arranging transport; a launch farther away needs a longer day.

- [Mauritius Tandem](https://www.mauritius-tandem-paragliding.com/)

## Le Bistrot de la Poste

French bistro at Nautica Commercial Centre, La Preneuse, Rivière Noire. The user confirmed this is the venue previously called The Post. Its claimed restaurant listing gives dinner Monday–Saturday and Sunday closure. The map now uses its listed coordinates, and the restaurant is available to the planner.

- [Restaurant listing](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g951345-d15575919-Reviews-Le_Bistrot_de_la_Poste-Riviere_Noire.html)
- [Menu and location](https://newhorizon.mu/fr/articles/where-to-eat-in-mauritius-6-must-visit-restaurants-in-the-west)
- [Map coordinates](https://mauritius.worldplaces.me/fr/view-place/83307362-le-bistrot-de-la-poste.html)

## Legend Hill

Hotel restaurants & bar. A hillside hotel in Rivière Noire with BVIEW for bistro dining, Le 1812 for a gourmet meal and the Santoshia poolside bar. Choose the restaurant when booking and confirm access for non-residents. A restaurant reservation does not automatically include a pool or spa day.

- [Legend Hill brochure](https://www.legendhill-resort.com/download/legendhillbrochureen.pdf)

## Kind Coffee

Specialty coffee café. A specialty coffee shop in La Gaulette, started by Taylor Holl and Willow-River Tonkin. It makes a coffee stop on the way to or from Le Morne. Allow travel time from Tamarin if you are going just for coffee.

- [Founder interview](https://magazine.coffee/blog/1/6718/coffee-culture-takes-flight-in-mauritius)

## Moustache Bistro

Bistro & wine bar. A French bistro and wine bar on Royal Road in La Mivoie, Tamarin. A local dinner option after an outing. Check the current opening days before making it your dinner plan.

- [Restaurant listing](https://moustachebistro.restaurant.mu/en)

## Sketch

Breakfast & lunch café. A daytime café on Royal Road in La Mivoie, Tamarin. Its published hours are 8am–4pm daily, with the kitchen closing at 3pm. Use it for breakfast or lunch; it is not a dinner stop.

- [Sketch hours](https://www.sketchmauritius.com/contact)

## Emba Filao

Beach restaurant. A restaurant directly on Le Morne public beach, beside LUX* Le Morne, serving seafood and Mauritian dishes. Plan it as a beach lunch stop and check current service hours before travelling specifically for a meal.

- [LUX Island Resorts](https://www.luxislandresorts.com/media/7145/integrated-annual-report-lir.pdf)
- [Current restaurant listing](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Restaurant_Review-g488105-d7023362-Reviews-Emba_Filao-Le_Morne.html)
