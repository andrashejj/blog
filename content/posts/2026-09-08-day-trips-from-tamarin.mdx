Every visitor asks the same three questions: how long does it take, do we need a car, and is there food there. So here is the list I keep sending people, with those answers already in it.

We live in Tamarin, on the west coast. Everything below is reachable from here and back in a day. Hit EN or DE to switch the table, tick the rows you refuse to miss, and the planner at the bottom will lay them out across however many days you have.

<TamarinGuide />

A hire car is about $25 a day. That's less than one taxi run to Chamarel and back, and roughly half the rows above need one, so unless you're staying inside Tamarin the whole time, rent something on day one.

The guided water trips are the exception. They all cost around $100 per person, they all include pickup, and they all leave early. Dolphins go out around 7am and whales only run from roughly July to October, so if that's the reason you came, check the month before you book the flights.

Ebony Forest and the south coast drive can be combined, but it makes for a long day and you'll rush the forest. Same for Le Morne: the hike and the beach are two different days, not one.
