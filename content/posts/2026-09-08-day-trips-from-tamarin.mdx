We live in Tamarin, on the west coast of Mauritius. I've put together a list of day trips from here, along with a few places to eat. Each entry covers how much time you'll need, whether you need a car, and what's available for food.

The map shows all the places. You can filter the list, select anything you'd like to do, and use the planner to spread it across your stay. The list and planner are available in English and German.

<TamarinGuide />

Most of the inland trips need a car. I use about $25 a day as a rough car-hire budget. Boat prices depend on the route, group size and operator. Dolphin and whale trips usually leave early. Check the meeting point and whether pickup is included when you book.

I'd keep a full day for the south coast drive. Ebony Forest can be a half-day outing, depending on the walk you choose. The Le Morne hike and the beach are also better on separate days. After an early boat trip, I'd keep the afternoon free.
