We live in Tamarin, on the west coast of Mauritius, and this is the list of day trips I'd hand you if you came to stay. Each entry covers how much time you'll need, whether you need a car and what there is to eat. A few places to eat made the list in their own right.

Everything is on the map. Filter the list, pick what you fancy, and the planner will spread it across your stay. Both the list and the planner work in English and German.

<TamarinGuide />

Most of the inland trips need a car. I work on roughly $25 a day for car hire. Boat prices depend on the route, the size of your group and the operator. Dolphin and whale trips usually leave early, and when you book, check the meeting point and whether pickup is included.

Don't cram. Give the south coast drive a full day. Ebony Forest can be a half-day outing, depending on which walk you pick. The Le Morne hike and the beach are better on separate days. And after an early boat trip, keep the afternoon free.
