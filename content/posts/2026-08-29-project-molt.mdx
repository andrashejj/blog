Estelle has started her internship with Sunset Duckies. I did not want to give her the usual useless internship assignment to “help with the brand.” Project Molt gives her a proper piece of work. Every phase has a deadline and something she can hand over at the end of it.

The brief is lopsided today. Healthy granola has a fairly complete concept. A first surfboard has a sketch. Everyday wear and surf wear still say “not yet ideated.” If we ran the interviews tomorrow, granola would probably win because it is the only option with enough detail to picture.

The 7 September deadline is for four equally real options. Each needs a customer, a problem, a use around surfing, a quality promise, materials or ingredients, and a provisional price. Maker calls wait until then.

Sunset Duckies already has a good duck, a group of kids who care about it, parents who help, and a real place behind the story. A few friendly sales would be easy. They could also fool us. We could print the logo on an ordinary product, sell it to friendly parents, and mistake loyalty for demand.

The club cannot become a captive customer list. If a product only sells because a parent wants to support the kids, the product failed. A surfer should choose it because it is useful, well made, and worth the price. I will not ask anyone to overlook bad taste, weak stitching, or the wrong price because the product helped the club.

Between 8 and 18 September, we will put all four ideas in front of the founding families, the kids, at least five buyers outside the club, and one possible maker for each concept. Refusals and price objections go into the notes alongside the nice comments. We will use those records to choose which product goes forward.

We will make a small batch or sample and launch it alongside a surf competition in Tamarin at the end of October. The announcement and registration need to go out before the event. On the day, the product gets a visible price. After the launch, we will decide what happens next from sales, orders, refusals, and product problems. Likes and reach stay out of it.

One launch tells us very little. From November through February we need a second product cycle with actual costs, margin, repeat orders, and returns. People in Tamarin should be able to run that cycle without waiting for me. We will also show the plan to surfers in at least two communities outside Mauritius and see which parts still make sense there.

Estelle starts with the four product sheets. They are due on 7 September; everyday wear and surf wear still need an actual product idea.
