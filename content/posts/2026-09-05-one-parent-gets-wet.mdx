I came back from summer missing the Duckies and carrying a much bigger belly. Looking down, I think both the belly and the club could use some training, so let's get down to business.

Sunset Duckies starts again on Friday 11 September, 4-6pm in Tamarin Bay. The kickoff is open to everyone. New duckies get their first session free.

After that, regular Monday and Friday training is for members only. Membership costs Rs 3,000 per semester for one child or Rs 5,000 for a family.

The money helps us run the club, but the fee is also a commitment. We take the sessions seriously. The duckies need to show up and get in the water, and the parents need to be part of it too.

## Parents get wet

We had too many gnarly situations last semester. From now on, every participating family must have at least one parent or guardian in the water for the full session. The rule starts immediately, including the free first session for a new duckie.

Standing on the beach is no longer enough. If your kid is in the water with the club, you are in there too.

This makes the commitment physical, which is probably the right test. It is easy to say you support a kids' surf club while staying dry. It feels different when you are standing waist-deep in shorebreak for two hours on a Friday afternoon.

## Importing Swiss talent

Estelle, the daughter of a good friend of mine from Switzerland, is travelling to Mauritius for an internship with Sunset Duckies. She will work on the brand and contact every family that joins the club.

Yes, Sunset Duckies is now officially importing Swiss talent. This got out of hand somewhere around the first duck sticker.

We are also planning the next Sunset Duckies competition for October. The date and format are not locked yet. First we get the crew back into the water, parents included.
