I missed the Duckies over the summer. Looking down, I think my belly could also use some training, so let's get down to business.

We start again on Friday 11 September, 4-6pm in Tamarin Bay. Everyone can join the kickoff, and the first session is free for new duckies.

After the kickoff, the Monday and Friday sessions will be for members. Membership is Rs 3,000 per semester for one child or Rs 5,000 for a family.

The fee is part of the commitment. We are putting time into running the club, and I want the kids and parents to take it seriously too: turn up, train, and help in the water.

## One parent in the water

Last semester we had a few situations with too many kids in the water and not enough adults. I should have made the rule clearer earlier.

From now on, every family must have at least one parent or guardian in the water for the full session. Watching from the beach does not count. This also applies to the free first session.

I know this asks more from the parents, but we are a small volunteer club and we need enough adults around the kids. More parents in the water means more eyes on them when things get messy.

## Estelle is joining us

Estelle is the daughter of a good friend of mine from Switzerland. She is travelling to Mauritius for an internship with Sunset Duckies and will help us with the brand. She will also speak with everyone who joins the club, so don't be surprised when she gets in touch.

We are also planning the next Sunset Duckies competition for October. The date and format are not fixed yet. We will share them when they are.
